# Reference scenarios (EXAMPLE — replace with your own, STAR format)

## Q: "Walk us through a large identity consolidation you led."
- Situation: Multi-entity merger, several AD forests, 200+ applications, hard cutover date.
- Action: Single identity workstream owner; trust teardown plan, coexistence period, app-by-app migration waves, TSA exit.
- Impact: Cut over on schedule; zero P1 identity incidents at exit; one directory, one governance model.
- Say next: "The hard part was never the tooling — it was sequencing trusts so nobody lost access on a Monday."
- Watch out: They may ask about rollback — have the coexistence-window answer ready.

## Q: "How do you evaluate a PAM vendor?"
- Criteria: discovery coverage, session isolation, rotation breadth, API/automation surface, HA/DR, licensing, agent footprint.
- Impact metric: % of privileged accounts vaulted in 90 days; mean time to rotate.
- Watch out: Ask them to demo break-glass and audit export, not just the dashboard.
